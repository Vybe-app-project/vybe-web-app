Delete Your Vybe Account

Last updated: July 28, 2026

You can request deletion directly in the Vybe app. This is the fastest and most secure method because you are already signed in to the account.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Vybe and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the deletion notice and confirm that you want to permanently delete the account. Complete any authentication step shown in the app.

After confirmation, Vybe signs you out and begins processing the deletion. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

What deletion covers

Account deletion removes or de-identifies personal information associated with your Vybe account from active systems, including:

  • account credentials, profile details, preferences, and push-notification identifiers;
  • posts, comments, photos, videos, social connections, saves, and community activity associated with your account;
  • workout, fitness, health-goal, nutrition, meal, hydration, body-measurement, achievement, and progress information; and
  • chat and support information associated with the account, subject to the limited exceptions below.

Account deletion is permanent. Download or save anything you want to keep before confirming. Content that another person copied or shared outside Vybe is not controlled by Vybe and may remain with that person or service.

Limited retention

Vybe may retain narrowly limited information where necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, protect users, or enforce agreements. Residual copies may remain in protected backups until those backups are overwritten through the ordinary backup cycle. Retained information is not used to keep providing the deleted account with the Service.

If you cannot use the in-app control

If you cannot sign in, the app is unavailable, or the deletion control does not work, send a private support request. Begin the message with Account deletion request.

Use the email address associated with the account and include the username, if known, plus a brief explanation of why you cannot use the in-app control. Never send your password, authentication code, full contact list, government ID, health records, or payment information. We may ask for limited information needed to verify that you control the account. We will process verified requests within the time required by applicable law.

Questions

For questions about deletion or privacy, use the private support form or read the Vybe Privacy Policy.
